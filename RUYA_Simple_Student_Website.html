From Decision to Action
A New Member Orientation Toolkit

01 · The scenario

Meet Noura.

Noura is a new member of RUYA, a university student innovation team developing digital ideas to support students.

Her first assignment is to choose a suitable digital idea for first-year university students.

Alternative A

Digital Student Guide

A digital guide designed to help first-year students.

Alternative B

Interactive Welcome Activity

An interactive activity to welcome first-year students.

Noura is unsure which idea is more suitable. She needs a rational way to compare them.

02 · Tool 1

Smart Decision Card

Based on the Rational Decision-Making Model. Compare ideas using clear criteria instead of quick assumptions.

The five steps

  1. Identify Decision Criteria
  2. Generate Alternatives
  3. Evaluate Alternatives
  4. Select the Best Alternative
  5. Implement the Alternative

Four criteria

Usefulness
Ease of Use
Feasibility
Implementation Time

Rate each idea from 1 to 5. Base the decision on available information and actual student needs, not personal assumptions.

03 · Your turn

Compare the ideas.

Choose a rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) for each criterion and each alternative.

04 · Decision

The results.

Noura's model decision

In RUYA's scenario, Noura selects the Digital Student Guide, explains her choice to the team, and identifies the tasks, resources, and first actions needed to develop its proposal.

The model example uses 18/20 for the Guide and 14/20 for the Welcome Activity. Your ratings above are for practice and may lead to a different result.

05 · Tool 2

My Goal Tracker

The team approves the Digital Student Guide, but asks Noura to revise its proposal within three days.

Noura's goal

Complete all requested revisions to the Digital Student Guide proposal within three days while meeting the team's requirements.

Goal-Setting Theory: specific, challenging but attainable goals, commitment, and feedback.

Three-day plan

Track progress

0 of 3 tasks completed

06 · Reflection

From decision to action.

One assignment. Two practical tools.

Tool 1

Smart Decision Card

Noura compares alternatives and makes a rational decision using four criteria.

Tool 2

My Goal Tracker

She organizes three days of revisions, monitors progress, and uses feedback.

Expected outcome

Better-informed decisions, clearer planning, and improved task completion. In the model scenario, Noura finishes the revisions within three days.

These are expected outcomes for a hypothetical scenario, not measured results.
